This document maps out the copywriting decision-making logic of KJ's Copywriting System, moving from macro aims down to micro-tactical execution.

## System Rules & Objectives

1.  **Macro Aim:** Scale Content-Market Fit (CMF) to drive outbound leads (€250k–€1M in 90 days).
2.  **Micro Aim:** Optimize individual assets for CTR, emotional resonance, and conversion hook rates.
3.  **Subsystems and their Goals:**
    *   **Specific Conditions:** Set the initial parameters (e.g. constraints, context, environment) that dictate how the prospect views the problem.
    *   **Text-Subtext:** Speak to the prospect's hidden thoughts, managing what they read between the lines.
    *   **Liking-Relatability:** Establish rapport and similarity so the prospect identifies with the copy.
    *   **Pain-Confidence:** Pinpoint specific frustrations (Pain) and pair them with authoritative solutions (Confidence).
    *   **Problem Chains:** Walk the reader through the cascading consequences of leaving their core problem unsolved.
    *   **Urgency-Scarcity:** Drive immediate decisions based on real, logical limits (time or availability).
    *   **Concise-Curiosity:** Cut all fluff while inserting open loops that keep the reader hooked.
    *   **Synthesis:** Run recursive validation loops to score the entire copy block before publishing.
