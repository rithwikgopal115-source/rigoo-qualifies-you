## First Principles: The Engine of Persuasion

**Copy = Persuasive Writing Skill × Avatar Understanding × Generalised Marketing Skill**

### Persuasive Writing Skill
The ability to take the avatar understanding and reason through it using deductive and inductive reasoning that pushes to higher levels of abstraction. The avatar understanding is split into multiple sections, and multiple sections need a different line of inductive and deductive 'logic tree' or 'line of reasoning' to arrive at the right judgment. **THE MONEY IS IN THE JUDGEMENT.**

The end objective is to arrive at the right judgment about the target avatar. As the conditions deck is split into multiple parts, the system needs to arrive at judgment for each micro-part of the system that pushes the "avatar raw data" into a principled, scientific, standardized, grounded, sociological, cultural snapshot—a macro judgment about the target avatar that we can use to 'manipulate' them. The macro judgment comes from reasoning through (deductive and inductive) the micro judgments. 

This provides the Avatar Judgments for the actual writing and it influences the copy approach, the marketing strategy, and the nuances and things to take into consideration. 

### Generalised Marketing Skill
A process that uses the Avatar Judgments as an input, which creates the output of a marketing strategy.

So generalized marketing skill will produce Avatar understanding, which is fractal. What I mean by that is that the avatar understanding has layers:
*   **Deepest Layer:** The UGC and conditions deck.
*   **Middle Layer:** Micro Avatar Judgments (judgments about how to go about their life and conditions, using nuanced mental models and schools of thought like neuroscience, behaviorism, psychology, etc.).

When the model is retrieving Avatar understanding as a whole, it goes to the Avatar judgments first, goes into a line of questioning that makes it find the necessary avatar data that is more concrete in the deepest layers (e.g., a set of UGC, the slang words they use, or the limiting beliefs they have), and then just "copy-pastes" that into the context window and goes through a line of reasoning as to how to use it in the copy. 

It's a RAG system. **(THE MONEY IS IN THE LINE OF REASONING).** Essentially, we are fine-tuning an AI.

### Avatar Understanding Formula
**Avatar Understanding = Research Accuracy × Classification Quality (In a way that the signal is not lost)** 

*   **Research Accuracy:** Inputs from the right avatar that reveals their Specific Conditions.
*   **Classification Quality:** Classifying their Specific Conditions to the right psychological categories.
