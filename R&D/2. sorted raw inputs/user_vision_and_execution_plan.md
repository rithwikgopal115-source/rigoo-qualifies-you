# The Vision & Execution Plan

So we have to build the node-based architecture, right? For the Research Agent, the Writing Agent, and the Creator Voice Agent. There's a Humanizer element to the Creator Voice Agent as well; it's built-in. It looks at the token prediction patterns of AI writing and then just disrupts it to make it sound really, really human. When it all comes together and is carefully put into an AI, it will output what it should output.

To build the audience awareness stage, you need to have a database of what each audience awareness stage is about. You need a global MD file detailing each audience awareness stage. Then you need to know how to approach different audience awareness stages, how it is dependent on multiple different niches, and how it is dependent on other nodes. Also, when talking about platform and format constraints, you usually know which audience awareness stages will be on which platform. There is a lot of nuance to it; it varies from brand to brand.

When talking about funnel context and brand voice, that is exactly what I meant. With brand voice, there's the existing writing of the creator and the personality of the creator. How do they write? What is their taste? From that, we produce the copy. We take the message and fit it into that brand context and the funnel context. Then we finally write it. It applies that personality, taste, and judgment on top. All of this creates a lead and angle selection. It outputs multiple leads and angles, and selects the best from it. Node 5 is the Writing Agent that takes all of this context and actually writes the draft (using a model like Sonnet).

To come up with the lead and angle selection, it takes high taste and high judgment. To judge the funnel context and everything else requires high taste and judgment. The selection of the angle and lead must be the output of a process of thinking and reasoning from Layer 2. Layer 2 sits on top of this MVP and is full of the best ways of thinking: neuroscience, cognitive psychology, sociology, etc. From that database, the system thinks in a specific way to come to the lead and angle selection. You apply this reasoning to all nodes in varying levels, and then you come up with a marketing judgment, a marketing strategy, and a copy draft. The copy draft will be created by the Writing Agent. The marketing strategy will be created by the Marketing Agent. And the research will be taken care of by the Research Agent.

If you're wondering what Layer 2 is: it is the amalgamation of mental models and thinking patterns of the greatest marketers who have achieved a holistic education about the nature of reality. They understand how humans work, how content works, and how to grab someone's attention if they know their psychographics and demographics. They can come up with dynamic marketing plans based on this literature.
