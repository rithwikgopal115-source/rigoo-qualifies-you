Information Architecture Brief

The neurosymbolic 3-layer structural blueprint for the Influence Accelerator System, moving from foundational conditions to advanced execution "taste."

System Overview

The Influence Accelerator processes inputs through a progressive 3-tier hierarchy. Data enters at the base level (Conditions & Avatar Attributes), gets synthesized through the logic layer (Human Sociality & Evolutionary Psychology), and finally executes via the node-based decision matrix (Taste & Nuance). The ultimate goal is maximum accuracy in generating the Marketing.md, Avatar_Internal.md, and the final Stimuli/Ascension pathway.

Layer 1: The Inner Layer Conditions & Data

The foundational bedrock of the system. This layer acts as the primary knowledge graph, holding all deterministic rules and core inputs.

KJ's Conditions Deck

  • Specific Conditions
  • Text & Subtext Mapping
  • Liking vs. Relatability
  • Pain vs. Confidence Mechanics
  • Problem Chains & Agitation
  • Urgency & Scarcity Frameworks
  • Concise/Curiosity Triggers

Avatar Architecture

  • The 28 core psychographic traits
  • Demographic baseline
  • Current state vs. desired outcome
  • Fundamental worldview constraints

Database Retrieval: Direct key-value lookups and structured knowledge graph (KG) querying for specific conditions triggered by the Avatar inputs.

Layer 2: The Judgment Layer Reasoning & Strategy

The neuro-engine. This layer applies deep academic, psychological, and evolutionary theory to the raw data provided by Layer 1 to determine how to position the stimulus.

Human Sociality Engine

  • 16 Levels of Evolutionary Psychology
  • Neuroscience-backed cognitive biases
  • Academic research on human behavior

Chain of Thought Processing

  • Generates positioning hypotheses
  • Analyzes: "How do we elicit action X from Avatar Y?"
  • Synthesizes Layer 1 data into actionable strategies

Database Retrieval: Semantic vector search (RAG) against the deep academic libraries to pull relevant psychological framing based on Layer 1's active conditions.

↓
Layer 3: The Taste Layer Nuance & Execution

The masterclass execution layer. This is where clinical strategy becomes compelling, nuanced marketing. It relies on a complex, node-based decision tree derived from expert transcripts.

Nuanced Decision Matrix

  • Derived from masterclass transcripts & expert breakdowns
  • Node-based execution pathways (Micro-level decision trees)
  • Sentence-level nuance and structural flow

Execution & Outputs

  • Integration of Creator Brand Voice
  • Funnel & Pathway specific mechanics
  • Final generation of Marketing.md and Avatar_Internal.md
  • Direct prompt passing to the Writing Agent for Stimulus creation

Database Retrieval: Graph-based traversal through expert decision nodes to determine the exact sequence of arguments and tonal shifts required for the final copy.

Data Flow & Optimization

The primary engineering focus moving forward is the backend mechanics linking these layers:

  • Recursion: How effectively the system loops through Layer 2 to refine its judgment before passing to Layer 3.
  • Data Pulling: The exact orchestration of how Layer 1 pulls from the KG vs. how Layer 2 pulls from Vector DBs.
  • Classification: The weighting algorithms that decide which psychological condition is most dominant for the specific Avatar at this exact point in the funnel.
